# Contributing to Flashpoint

Flashpoint is built by FRC Team 6829, Ignite Robotics, and we'd like it to work for your team too. Here's how to help, and what to expect from us.

## Questions

Ask in [Q&A discussions](https://github.com/IgniteRobotics/flashpoint/discussions/categories/q-a): how to install, import logs, read a view, or set up your robot. Answers there stay searchable for the next team, so please ask there rather than by email or in an issue.

## Bugs and feature requests

Use the [issue forms](https://github.com/IgniteRobotics/flashpoint/issues/new/choose). The bug form asks for `flashpoint --version`, your OS and Python, the `flashpoint doctor` output and the kinds of logs involved, which is what we need to reproduce a problem.

## Logs

Logs from other teams' robots are the most useful thing you can give us: every team logs a little differently, and Flashpoint only gets better at reading them by seeing them.

- **Never attach `.wpilog` or `.hoot` files to a public issue or discussion.** They hold your team's match data.
- Share them **privately** instead: say in your issue or discussion that you have logs to share, and a maintainer will reply with how to send them, usually a Google Drive or Dropbox link shared only with them. We use them to test Flashpoint and don't publish them or pass them on.

## Pull requests

**Pull requests are accepted from collaborators only.** IgniteRobotics closes pull requests from anyone else automatically, every day, because our students earn write access through training. It isn't a judgement on your change.

If you'd like to fix something:
- open an issue first, describing the problem and the fix you have in mind;
- or push the fix to a branch on your own fork and link it from the issue. A team member can bring it in with a `Co-authored-by` credit, as we did for #92.

Our developer guide covers building and testing from source: [developer guide](https://github.com/IgniteRobotics/flashpoint/blob/main/docs/guide/developer-guide.md).

## Response times

Flashpoint is run by volunteers, a mentor and students, around a robotics season.

- **During build season** (January to April) expect a first reply within a week. Competition weekends are slower.
- **The rest of the year,** usually within a few days.

Security problems have their own route: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Everyone taking part is expected to follow our [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
