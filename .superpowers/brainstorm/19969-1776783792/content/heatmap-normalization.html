Heatmap color scale — global or per-motor?

How should color intensity be assigned across motors?

A — Global scale (click to select)

All motors share one color scale. Red = highest W on the robot. A motor that barely ran stays blue even at its personal peak.

Note: intake row stays blue — you can tell it drew less than the drivetrain.

B — Per-motor scale (click to select)

Each motor's color spans its own min–max. Red = that motor's personal peak. Shows temporal pattern for every motor equally — but you can't compare intensity across rows.

Note: intake row is equally vivid — you can see its duty cycle but not that it barely draws power.
